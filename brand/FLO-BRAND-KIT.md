# FLO Brand System

## Brand architecture

- **FLO:** shared client-facing identity, guide and spoken shorthand.
- **OpenFootLab:** local office, physical service, manufacturing and human relationship.
- **FootLabOS:** operating system, monitoring protocol, Flight Sheet, infrastructure and Field Notes.

## Identity idea

The identity is built around a simple contrast: sophisticated technology behind the scenes, effortless human service in front. The FLO monogram is intentionally compact and direct. It can be spoken as “flow,” while its O represents the continuous observe-to-improve loop. The bright green is a signal of life, continuity and action—not decoration.

## Color system

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | FootLab Green | `#B7FF67` |
| Foundation | Operating Black | `#080C0E` |
| Surface | Engineered Graphite | `#11171A` |
| Border | System Line | `#273137` |
| Secondary text | Quiet Gray | `#94A0A6` |
| Primary light text | Human White | `#F4F7F8` |

Use green with restraint. It should identify the system, confirm an action or highlight the human outcome. Avoid flooding entire layouts with green.

## Typography

Use a modern grotesk sans-serif. Preferred production families: Inter, Helvetica Neue, Arial or a metrically compatible system sans-serif. Use heavy weights for short statements and regular weights for explanatory copy.

## Logo rules

- Keep clear space equal to one quarter of the FLO mark width.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline or add effects.
- Do not recolor the green.
- Do not place the mark on visually busy photography.
- At small sizes, use the FLO mark without the tagline.
- Always spell the brand `FootLabOS` with capital F, L and OS.

Canonical OpenFootLab web asset: `public/brand/footlabos-flo-mark-lime.svg`.

## Voice

FootLabOS and OpenFootLab speak calmly, directly and without hype. Lead with the client result. Technology remains supporting evidence.

Preferred:

- Built around the individual.
- Bilateral by design. Asymmetric by need.
- We absorb the complexity.
- Maximize healthy days.
- Small reads. Real signals. Your edge.

Avoid:

- AI-powered miracle language
- autonomous medical claims
- fear-based health messaging
- jargon that makes the client operate the technology
